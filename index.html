Why Choose Underground Boring?

Missile Drilling vs. Open Cut Trenching

Open-cut trenching can require cutting paved surfaces, disturbing landscaping, coordinating restoration work, and extending site disruption.

T&M Underground's trenchless methods create an underground pathway through small entry and exit areas, often reducing surface restoration compared with open-cut work. Actual site impact and turnaround depend on project conditions.

Fewer Surface Cuts

Trenchless methods can reduce the need to cut concrete or open a continuous surface trench.

Reduced Landscaping Impact

Small entry and exit areas help protect established lawns, pavers, and surrounding landscaping.

Quick Turnaround

Short residential bores can often be completed efficiently, subject to site conditions and utility coordination.

Equipment & Telemetry Tech

Our compact rigs and pneumatic moles allow us to operate in tight residential access zones without disturbing surrounding structures.

Pneumatic Piercing Moles

High-frequency reciprocating chisel heads that compact surrounding soil to create clean, un-caved utility tunnels specifically sized for 1" to 2" conduits.

• Shot Range: 10ft – 90ft
• Conduit Capacity: 1" – 2" HDPE / PVC
• Entry Footprint: 3ft x 2ft

Digital Sonde Locating

Electromagnetic tracking equipment provides real-time depth, pitch, and roll information to support accurate steering and help reduce cross-bore risk.

• Depth Accuracy: ±0.5 inches
• Pitch Precision: 0.1% slope

Compact HDD Boring Rigs

Rubber-tracked compact directional drills designed to fit through standard 36" residential gates, ideal for long 1" to 2" conduit runs.

• Gate Clearance: 36 inches
• Pullback Force: Up to 15,000 lbs
