Equipment & Telemetry Tech

Our compact rigs and pneumatic moles allow us to operate in tight residential access zones without disturbing surrounding structures.

Pneumatic Piercing Moles

High-frequency reciprocating chisel heads that compact surrounding soil to create clean, un-caved utility tunnels specifically sized for 1" to 2" conduits.

• Shot Range: 10ft – 90ft
• Conduit Capacity: 1" – 2" HDPE / PVC
• Entry Footprint: 3ft x 2ft

Digital Sonde Locating

Subsite and Digitrak electromagnetic tracking heads providing real-time depth, pitch, and roll telemetry to guarantee zero cross-bore strikes.

• Depth Accuracy: ±0.5 inches
• Pitch Precision: 0.1% slope

Compact HDD Boring Rigs

Rubber-tracked compact directional drills designed to fit through standard 36" residential gates, ideal for long 1" to 2" conduit runs.

• Gate Clearance: 36 inches
• Pullback Force: Up to 15,000 lbs
